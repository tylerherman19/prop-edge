WHAT THIS CAN'T SAY YET

READ THIS PART

Historical market lines are not in any free feed, so the 2025 test grades projections against real outcomes - not against 2025's lines. The honest statement that data supports: "this model is usually within X of the truth." The statement it cannot yet support: "this edge would have beaten 2025's market." That second claim starts accumulating from the week the collector went live, one graded week at a time.
