PROP EDGEWK -
THE BOARD ACCURACY METHOD

Loading the board - pulling the latest lines and projections.

Every prop is priced both ways, like a prediction market. The two percentages come from the player's own game log - recency-weighted, shrunk toward his position's league average when he's played few, then calibrated on 2025 results. Model pick marks the side his log favors. The estimate underneath is this week's Sleeper/ESPN projection, a separate signal - when the two disagree, you see both and make the call.

Edge is the default sort: hit-chance confidence times the model-vs-line disagreement, measured in the player's own spread. A big gap the player's log agrees with outranks a coin flip with a huge gap. Hit% and Gap sorts are still there when you want one signal at a time.

A big gap is a disagreement, not a guarantee. Nothing here is betting advice - it is arithmetic on free public data.
